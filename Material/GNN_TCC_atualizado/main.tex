\begin{resumo}
A detec\c{c}\~ao de fake news em redes sociais e tradicionalmente abordada por
processamento de linguagem natural (PLN), mas essa estrat\'egia perde
robustez frente a (i) modelos generativos capazes de produzir texto
gramaticalmente impec\'avel e (ii) postagens em idiomas n\~ao cobertos por
modelos pre-treinados. Este trabalho investiga em que condi\c{c}\~oes a
\textbf{topologia da propaga\c{c}\~ao} -- o padr\~ao com que uma not\'icia se
espalha pela rede -- pode complementar ou substituir a an\'alise textual.

Partimos de tr\^es perguntas de pesquisa: (RQ1) topologia carrega sinal
discriminante acima do que o texto da raiz da \'arvore de propaga\c{c}\~ao
fornece? (RQ2) sob quais condi\c{c}\~oes estruturais? (RQ3) \'e vi\'avel
detectar fake news \emph{apenas} pela topologia, em cen\'arios de texto
indispon\'ivel ou multil\'ingue?

Realizamos ablation sistem\'atica em tr\^es datasets (FakeNewsNet PolitiFact,
UPFD-PolitiFact, UPFD-GossipCop), comparando arquiteturas GCN, GAT e
GraphSAGE com tr\^es variantes de \emph{features} estruturais
($[is\_root]$, $[is\_root, grau\_norm]$, $[is\_root, grau\_norm, pos]$).
Os experimentos s\~ao validados com k-fold pareado, teste $t$ pareado
(\texttt{ttest\_rel}) e tamanho de efeito de Cohen ($d$).

Os achados centrais s\~ao bilaterais. \textbf{Positivo}: GraphSAGE com apenas
duas \emph{features} estruturais por n\'o atinge F1-macro = $0{,}810$ em
UPFD-GossipCop \emph{sem usar texto algum} (10 \emph{seeds}, $\sigma = 0{,}002$).
\textbf{Negativo}: a mesma arquitetura colapsa em UPFD-PolitiFact
(F1 $\approx 0{,}55$). A causa estrutural \'e identificada via Cohen's $d$:
GossipCop apresenta diferen\c{c}a entre fake e real de $d = +1{,}53$ no
\emph{branching factor} das cascatas; PolitiFact apresenta $|d| < 0{,}2$
em todas as m\'etricas avaliadas. A tese central -- formulada de forma
falsific\'avel -- \'e que modelos topol\'ogicos requerem diferen\c{c}a
estrutural entre classes da ordem de magnitude de um efeito m\'edio de
Cohen ($|d| \geq 0{,}5$) para serem efetivos.

Aplicamos os modelos persistidos sobre 168.463 posts do Bluesky em modo de
demonstra\c{c}\~ao (sem r\'otulos), confirmando que o classificador
estrutural produz distribui\c{c}\~ao de \emph{score} estatisticamente
equivalente entre portugu\^es e ingl\^es ($d = -0{,}057$), o que sustenta
a propriedade de independ\^encia lingü\'istica do mecanismo. Toda a
\'analise est\'a empacotada em uma aplica\c{c}\~ao web (FastAPI + Next.js)
que combina os classificadores textual e topol\'ogico via regra emp\'irica
derivada \emph{post-hoc} dos experimentos.

\textbf{Palavras-chave}: Fake news. Graph Neural Networks. UPFD. FakeNewsNet.
Bluesky. Cohen's d. Encodings posicionais.
\end{resumo}

\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
Fake-news detection in social media is traditionally tackled by Natural
Language Processing (NLP), but this strategy loses robustness against
(i) generative models capable of producing grammatically impeccable text
and (ii) posts in languages not covered by pre-trained models. This work
investigates under which conditions \textbf{propagation topology} -- the
pattern by which news spreads through the network -- can complement or
replace textual analysis.

We pose three research questions: (RQ1) does topology carry discriminative
signal beyond what the root-text of the propagation tree provides? (RQ2)
under which structural conditions? (RQ3) is it feasible to detect fake news
\emph{using topology only}, in scenarios with unavailable or multilingual
text?

We perform a systematic ablation on three datasets (FakeNewsNet PolitiFact,
UPFD-PolitiFact, UPFD-GossipCop), comparing GCN, GAT and GraphSAGE under
three structural feature variants. Experiments are validated with paired
k-fold, paired \texttt{ttest\_rel}, and Cohen's $d$ effect size.

Findings are bilateral. \textbf{Positive}: GraphSAGE with only two
per-node structural features achieves F1-macro = $0{.}810$ on UPFD-GossipCop
\emph{without using text at all} (10 seeds, $\sigma = 0{.}002$).
\textbf{Negative}: the same architecture collapses on UPFD-PolitiFact
(F1 $\approx 0{.}55$). The structural cause is identified via Cohen's $d$:
GossipCop shows a fake-vs-real difference of $d = +1{.}53$ on cascade
branching factor; PolitiFact shows $|d| < 0{.}2$ across all evaluated
metrics. Our central -- and falsifiable -- thesis is that topological
models require class-wise structural differences of at least a Cohen
medium-effect magnitude ($|d| \geq 0{.}5$) to be effective.

We apply the persisted models on 168,463 Bluesky posts in demonstration
mode (no labels), confirming that the structural classifier produces a
statistically equivalent score distribution between Portuguese and English
($d = -0{.}057$), supporting the linguistic independence of the mechanism.
The full analysis is packaged in a web application (FastAPI + Next.js)
that combines textual and topological classifiers via an empirical rule
derived \emph{post-hoc} from the experiments.

\textbf{Keywords}: Fake news. Graph Neural Networks. UPFD. FakeNewsNet.
Bluesky. Cohen's d. Positional encodings.
\end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
