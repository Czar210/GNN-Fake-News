\begin{resumo}
A detec\c{c}\~ao de fake news em redes sociais \'e tradicionalmente
abordada por processamento de linguagem natural (PLN), mas essa
estrat\'egia perde robustez frente a modelos generativos capazes de
produzir texto gramaticalmente impec\'avel e a postagens em idiomas
n\~ao cobertos por modelos pr\'e-treinados. Este trabalho investiga em
que condi\c{c}\~oes a \textbf{topologia da propaga\c{c}\~ao}, o padr\~ao
com que uma not\'icia se espalha pela rede, pode complementar ou
substituir a an\'alise textual. Tr\^es perguntas de pesquisa orientam o
trabalho: (RQ1) topologia carrega sinal discriminante acima do texto
da raiz da \'arvore de propaga\c{c}\~ao? (RQ2) sob quais condi\c{c}\~oes
estruturais? (RQ3) \'e vi\'avel detectar fake news \emph{apenas} pela
topologia?

Realizamos ablation sistem\'atica em tr\^es \emph{datasets} (FakeNewsNet
PolitiFact, UPFD-PolitiFact, UPFD-GossipCop), comparando GCN, GAT e
GraphSAGE com tr\^es variantes de \emph{features} estruturais. Os
experimentos s\~ao validados com k-fold pareado, teste $t$ pareado
(\texttt{ttest\_rel}) e tamanho de efeito de Cohen ($d$).

Os achados centrais s\~ao bilaterais. \textbf{Positivo}: GraphSAGE com
apenas duas \emph{features} estruturais por n\'o atinge F1-macro $=
0{,}810$ em UPFD-GossipCop \emph{sem usar texto algum} (10 \emph{seeds},
$\sigma = 0{,}002$). \textbf{Negativo}: a mesma arquitetura colapsa em
UPFD-PolitiFact (F1 $\approx 0{,}33$, abaixo da chance). A causa
estrutural \'e identificada via Cohen's $d$: GossipCop apresenta $d =
+1{,}53$ no \emph{branching factor} das cascatas; PolitiFact n\~ao
alcan\c{c}a efeito m\'edio em nenhuma m\'etrica avaliada ($|d| < 0{,}5$).
A tese central, deliberadamente falsific\'avel, \'e que modelos
topol\'ogicos requerem $|d| \geq 0{,}5$ em pelo menos uma m\'etrica de
cascata para serem efetivos. Como evid\^encia complementar para a parte
(b) da RQ3, o classificador estrutural produz, sobre subconjuntos
monol\'ingues de um \emph{snapshot} acad\^emico do Bluesky,
distribui\c{c}\~oes de \emph{score} estatisticamente equivalentes entre
portugu\^es e ingl\^es ($d = -0{,}057$). Os pesos persistidos no
Hugging Face Hub e a ferramenta web que integra os classificadores
est\~ao documentados no Ap\^endice~\ref{ap:aplicacao}.

\textbf{Palavras-chave}: Fake news. Graph Neural Networks. UPFD.
FakeNewsNet. Bluesky. Cohen's d. Encodings posicionais.
\end{resumo}

\begin{resumo}[Abstract]
\begin{otherlanguage*}{english}
Fake-news detection in social media is traditionally tackled by Natural
Language Processing (NLP), but this strategy loses robustness against
generative models capable of producing grammatically impeccable text
and against posts in languages not covered by pre-trained models. This
work investigates under which conditions \textbf{propagation topology},
the pattern by which news spreads through the network, can complement
or replace textual analysis. Three research questions guide the work:
(RQ1) does topology carry discriminative signal beyond the root-text
of the propagation tree? (RQ2) under which structural conditions?
(RQ3) is it feasible to detect fake news \emph{using topology only}?

We perform a systematic ablation on three datasets (FakeNewsNet
PolitiFact, UPFD-PolitiFact, UPFD-GossipCop), comparing GCN, GAT and
GraphSAGE under three structural feature variants. Experiments are
validated with paired k-fold, paired \texttt{ttest\_rel}, and Cohen's
$d$ effect size.

Findings are bilateral. \textbf{Positive}: GraphSAGE with only two
per-node structural features achieves F1-macro $= 0{.}810$ on
UPFD-GossipCop \emph{without using text at all} (10 seeds, $\sigma =
0{.}002$). \textbf{Negative}: the same architecture collapses on
UPFD-PolitiFact (F1 $\approx 0{.}33$, below chance). The structural
cause is identified via Cohen's $d$: GossipCop shows a fake-vs-real
difference of $d = +1{.}53$ on cascade branching factor; PolitiFact
does not reach a medium-effect size on any evaluated metric ($|d| <
0{.}5$). Our central and deliberately falsifiable thesis is that
topological models require $|d| \geq 0{.}5$ on at least one cascade
metric to be effective. As complementary evidence for RQ3 part (b),
the structural classifier produces, over monolingual subsets of an
academic Bluesky snapshot, statistically equivalent score
distributions between Portuguese and English ($d = -0{.}057$).
Persisted weights on the Hugging Face Hub and the web application
integrating the classifiers are documented in
Appendix~\ref{ap:aplicacao}.

\textbf{Keywords}: Fake news. Graph Neural Networks. UPFD. FakeNewsNet.
Bluesky. Cohen's d. Positional encodings.
\end{otherlanguage*}
\end{resumo}
